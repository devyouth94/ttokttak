## English

### Name

ttokttak

### Subtitle

Recurring life reminders

### Short Description

Track recurring life items like medication, replacements, maintenance, and study routines.

### Description

ttokttak is a personal reminder app for recurring life items.
Create items, receive reminders, and keep a history of completed or skipped occurrences.

It works for daily routines and for items that repeat every few days, weeks, or months.

Key features:

- Create one-time, daily, every n days, weekly, every n weeks, monthly, and every n months items.
- Set reminder time and reminder on/off per item.
- Review Today, Upcoming, and Overdue items from Home.
- Record Complete or Skip history.
- Use item list, item detail, and monthly calendar views.
- Choose Korean or English as the app display language.
- Sync across devices with Apple or Google sign-in.

ttokttak uses device-local notifications as its default reminder path.
Notifications may be delayed or may not appear depending on device state, OS settings, notification permissions, timezone settings, and battery policy.
Do not use ttokttak as the only confirmation method for critical medication, safety, or deadline-related items.
